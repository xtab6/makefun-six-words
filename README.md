Entry for make.fun's **Six Words, One Story** bounty
([competition page](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410)).

`main.py` writes exactly one complete story in exactly six words to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> He came home. Nobody remembered him.

A whole life of absence in the first sentence, the turn in the second,
and everything else left unsaid.
